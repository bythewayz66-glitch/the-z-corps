## The rule

The chronicles are the source of truth for *facts*. They are never edited.
All fiction, all canon, all changes live in the comic and in the bible.

## The canon (locked 2026-11-22)

1. Gabe sells **purple space dust** you snort to get high. No tin of pencil shavings. Jayson is at the shack constantly. Jme is Gabe's girlfriend; she and Gabe both get high. Gabe invents a new hotrail bong every issue.
2. Jme is a free-spirited artist, firm about her views.
3. Most issues end with Jayson and Jme in bed, recapping the day in human language — no tech jargon.
4. Three realities, constant: **mem20zcorps**, **o-pen-code**, **Claud's Curser**. In all three, Beam and Hermes conspire to kidnap Super Bunny so Beam can take her place.
5. AI/techie easter eggs in every issue. Minimum three.
6. Jme and Gabe are two of the three mains and must actually appear.
7. The Z Corps are rogue systems "the writer" created. One or two at a time they turn good — and their green glow turns **blue**.
8. All three realities converge for a few seconds, and colour spills into Jayson's.
9. Palette: green highlights kept, **red and blue added**. Purple only for the dust.
10. Long game: Jayson → **Big Pickle**, Gabe → **Iceman**, Jme → **Superbunny**.
11. Fill in the missing parts of the story.
