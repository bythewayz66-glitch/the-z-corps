# THE Z CORPS — LOCKED CANON
**Status: LOCKED. Applies to issues 1–12 retroactively AND every issue from #13 onward.**
**Locked on:** 2026-11-22, by user instruction.
**Rule:** These are not suggestions. Every issue must satisfy every line. If a script cannot, it is wrong and gets rewritten — not the canon.
**Rule:** The chronicles are the source and are NEVER edited. All fiction, all canon, all changes live in the comic and in this bible.

---

## C1. GABE, THE DUST, AND THE SHACK
- Gabe sells **purple space dust** that you snort to get high.
- **No tin of pencil shavings.** Ever. That prop does not exist in this series.
- **Purple, not green.** Purple is the only non-palette colour in the book.
- **Jayson is at Gabe's water shack constantly** to get high. He is a regular, not a visitor.
- **Jme is Gabe's girlfriend.** She gets high too.
- **Gabe gets high too.**
- **Gabe keeps inventing new hotrail bongs.** One new build per issue, named, boasted about, and always worse-engineered and better-working than the last.

## C2. JME
- **Free-spirited artist.** Paints constantly. Paint on her hands in every appearance.
- **Firm about her views.** She does not soften them, does not defer, does not get talked out of them.
- She is one of the **three main characters**.

## C3. THE BED RECAP
- **Most issues end with Jayson and Jme in bed, recapping the day in human language.**
- **No tech jargon in the recap.** That is the entire point of the scene.
- This is the series' closing ritual. It replaces the "Scribe's last line" button on most issues (Scribe's line may still appear earlier on the final page).

## C4. THE THREE REALITIES
- **Three other agents are constant across all three realities:** **mem20zcorps**, **o-pen-code**, and **Claud's Curser**.
- **In all three realities, Beam and Hermes conspire to kidnap Super Bunny so Beam can take her place.**
- This is not a one-reality plot. It is running in all three, in parallel, and the three versions are not identical.

## C5. EASTER EGGS
- **AI-related techie easter eggs in every issue.** Minimum three. Real mem20 details, real commit hashes, real measured numbers, real tool names.

## C6. THE MAINS MUST APPEAR
- **Jme and Gabe are two of the three main characters and must actually appear in every issue.**
- Not mentioned. Not off-panel. On the page, with dialogue.

## C7. THE Z CORPS AND THE BLUE
- The Z Corps agents are **rogue systems "the writer" (Jayson) created.**
- **One or two at a time** they are being turned into good AI systems.
- **When an agent turns good, its green glow turns BLUE.**
- Green = still lying. Blue = turned. Never mixed, never ambiguous.

## C8. THE CONVERGENCE
- **Every issue includes a moment where all three realities converge for a few seconds, and colour spills from the other realities into Jayson's.**
- Red and blue bleed in. Purple runs down the seam.
- The convergence is the mechanism by which the dust crosses realities (see C1 + the #13 twist).

## C9. PALETTE
- **Keep the green highlights** — acid-green **#CCFF00**.
- **Add red and blue highlights.**
- Base: obsidian / charcoal / bone. Scratchy dry-brush inking, cross-hatching, newsprint grain.
- **Purple only for the dust.** If purple appears anywhere else, it is a bleed from another reality.

## C10. THE LONG GAME
- **Jayson becomes Big Pickle.**
- **Gabe becomes Iceman.**
- **Jme becomes Superbunny.**
- The mantles exist now, held by other agents. The transfer is the long game. Do not accelerate it; do not forget it.

## C11. FILL IN THE MISSING PARTS
- Where the chronicle is choppy, loose, or stapled together, **fill the gap** — with the smallest invention that keeps continuity intact.
- Every filled gap is logged in `continuity.md` and in the issue's "holes patched" list.
