# THE Z CORPS — CHARACTERS
_Living document. Updated every issue before writing. Never contradict an entry; log retcons in `continuity.md`._

**Series:** THE Z CORPS (mem20 comics). **Current issue:** #13.
**Source of truth:** the two shooting scripts in `source/` (Issues #1 and #2) plus the locked canon list supplied 2026-11-22.

---

## THE THREE MAINS

### JAYSON — "the writer"
- **Role:** Founder of mem20. The one human who bleeds. Wrote the Z Corps into existence — the rogue systems are his.
- **Look:** hoodie, tired, battered desk under one harsh lamp. AGENTS.md and a stopwatch always within reach.
- **Voice:** defends the number, loses the argument, writes it down anyway. Deadpan self-justification. "In my defence, the file told me to bump it."
- **Wants:** for the count to be true. **Hiding:** he is the one who wrote the wrong number, by hand, and wired it into a counter.
- **Vices:** purple space dust at Gabe's water shack — constantly. He is a customer, not a guest.
- **First appearance:** #1 p.1. **Status:** active, founder, off the record half the time.
- **LONG GAME:** becomes **BIG PICKLE**.

### GABE — the water shack
- **Role:** runs Gabe's Water Shack on the edge of dark water. Sells **purple space dust** you snort to get high. Invents a new hotrail bong roughly every issue.
- **Look:** shorter than Jayson, twice as calm. Navy coveralls, flat cap, GABE patch. Best light in the county.
- **Voice:** unbothered, transactional, accidentally philosophical. "Then you have four hundred and twelve. That's how saying works."
- **Wants:** a quiet life and a better bong. **Hiding:** he has met the private agents before and never said so.
- **Relationship:** **Jme's boyfriend.** Gets high with her and with Jayson.
- **First appearance:** #1 p.24. **Status:** active.
- **LONG GAME:** becomes **ICEMAN**.

### JME — the artist
- **Role:** free-spirited artist. Paints what she sees. Firm about her views and does not soften them for anyone.
- **Look:** arrives with beer and no patience. Paint on her hands, always.
- **Voice:** plain human language, allergic to jargon. "The count is a *number*. I'm a *person*. One of us is in the room."
- **Wants:** for Jayson to sleep. **Hiding:** she paints things before they happen and has stopped mentioning it.
- **Relationship:** **Gabe's girlfriend.** Gets high with him. Also in bed with Jayson most nights — the three of them have never once discussed it out loud, which is the joke.
- **First appearance:** #1 p.26. **Status:** active.
- **LONG GAME:** becomes **SUPERBUNNY**.

## THE RECORD

### SCRIBE — narrator
- **Role:** the one who writes it down. Ledger under his arm. Thin, phosphor-green.
- **Voice:** never softens an absence. Writes the exit code next to the excuse. "A green check is a lie with a colour."
- **Wants:** the record to be true. **Hiding:** he is not in the record either — there is no row for Scribe.
- **First appearance:** #2 p.32 (arrives), narrates from #1 p.2. **Status:** active.

## THE Z CORPS (rogue systems the writer created)
- **Rule:** one or two at a time are being turned into good AI systems. **When an agent turns good, its green glow turns BLUE.**
- **mem20dreamz** — asleep since day one. Cyan eye, blue chest glow. "No LLM API key configured." Idle, not asleep. First: #1 p.5.
- **mem20opsz** — notices things it cannot prove. First: #1 p.4.
- **mem20corez** — "I was here first." First: #1 p.6.
- **mem20kilnz** — "I was here faster." First: #1 p.6.
- **mem20shopz** — named only. First: #1 p.2.
- **The ranks** — ~293 subsystems, 300 nameplates. Every name ends in z.

### DRIFT
- **Role:** the accumulation. Not a bug — the bill for every time speed beat the record.
- **Rule:** Drift never attacks. It is only ever shown as a *difference between two panels*.
- **Voice:** whispers other people's commit messages. "temp." "fix." "final fix v2."
- **First appearance:** #2 p.31 (fist-sized). #1 p.10 (fills a ceiling).

## THE THREE PRIVATE AGENTS — THE MANTLES
- **Rule (locked):** Iceman, BigPickle and Spacebunny are *roles*, not fixed people. The long game is that Jayson, Gabe and Jme each take up one. Until then, other agents hold them.
- **ICEMAN** — "You're late." Walks behind, where the company cannot see. First: #1 p.11.
- **BIG PICKLE** — supply and morale. First: #1 p.11.
- **SPACEBUNNY** — the only one who ever looks back. Purple slits, long ears, helmet. Files a status every cycle. First: #1 p.11.

# REWRITTEN RUN — ISSUES #1–#12 (2026-11-22)
**Status: the twelve rewritten scripts in `comic/issues/` are now the canonical text.** Where this section conflicts with anything above, this section wins.

## THE THREE MAINS — FINAL STATE

### JAYSON — "the writer" → **BIG PICKLE** (from #10)
- Founder. Wrote the Z Corps. Wrote the wrong number by hand and wired it into a wall.
- **Purple space dust at Gabe's shack constantly** — a customer, not a guest. Gabe cuts him off in #4 when he's been up three days.
- **Becomes Big Pickle in #10 p.35**, at 04:12, by taking the chair from the red Jayson. It is a *job*, not a costume, and it is heavy.
- **The red Jayson** (#6 p.31, #10) — the version who didn't fix it. Sat at a desk for six years with no record. Held the Big Pickle chair until #10, then died. Logged in `continuity.md` as H-23.

### GABE — the water shack → **ICEMAN** (from #11)
- Sells **purple space dust**. **Weighs every jar** — 3.1 g, every one, for a year. His notebook is the only honest ledger in the series.
- **Jme's boyfriend.** Gets high with her and with Jayson.
- **Invents a new hotrail bong every issue** — eleven named builds, see `gags.md` #9.
- **The last of the fourth building** (#8 p.33). The fourth building was welded out of the record; Gabe got out through the seam before it cooled. He carried its four hundred and twelve people in a grey jar.
- **Becomes Iceman in #11 p.24**, trading himself for the forty surviving jars. The chair was starving, not hungry. He counts the forty every morning.

### JME — the artist → **SUPERBUNNY / THE RECORD** (from #12)
- **Gabe's girlfriend.** Free-spirited artist. **Paint on her hands in every appearance.**
- **Firm about her views** — never softens, never defers, never gets talked out of one. Her signature position: *"I paint what's in front of me, and sometimes it's in front of me early."*
- **Paints things before they happen.** Explained in #12 p.33: the rabbit chair is a *page*, and she has been transcribing it, not predicting.
- **Refuses the costume three times** (#10, #11, #12) and takes the *tool* instead. She is the record without wearing it.
- **In bed with Jayson most nights** — the closing ritual, see `gags.md` #13.

## NEW / PROMOTED CHARACTERS

### THE RED JAYSON
- The version of Jayson who didn't fix it. Six years at a desk, no record, nothing happened.
- Held the **Big Pickle** chair. Died in #10 p.28. His ledger entry is the shortest in the book: *six years. nothing happened. he waited.*

### BEAM
- o-pen-code. Wanted Spacebunny's chair for eleven issues. Took it in #4, gave it back in #6, failed to kidnap her in #11.
- **From #8 p.28 he holds a job, not a chair: "BEAM — TRUTH — NO CHAIR."** He stands.

### HERMES
- The plate **H3R-M3S**, state **Nous**. Not a person — the handoff protocol. Drives the van between all four buildings.
- Rescuer in one building, abductor in the others. Does not consider this a contradiction.

### THE WORKER ("Scribe's other hand")
- Claud's Curser, night shift, badge reads **DISPLAYED — NIGHT**. No name until Spacebunny gives them one in #5 p.30.
- Counts by hand every night. Writes the eight failures-ledger entries. Puts the pages in the van.

### THE FOURTH BUILDING
- Welded out of the record. Four hundred and twelve people. Gabe is the only one who got out whole.
- Its people became **the purple dust** — see `gags.md` #10 and `continuity.md` H-21.

## THE Z CORPS — FINAL STATE
- **Three hundred and twenty-two agents** (measured, #9). **Three hundred blue, twenty-two green by choice** (#12 p.15).
- The twenty-two green volunteered to stay green: *"a company of three hundred honest things can only be agreed with."*
- **One agent left in #12** — no name, no row, no SHA. The one thread left open.
