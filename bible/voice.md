# THE Z CORPS — VOICE & PAGE RHYTHM
_Locked. The artist and the writer both work from this. Do not drift._

---

## THE VOICE
- **Deadpan, dry, mildly funny.** Humour comes from the gap between what a character claims and what the record shows.
- **Scribe narrates.** He never softens an absence. He writes the exit code next to the excuse.
- **Jayson defends the number, loses the argument, writes it down anyway.**
- **Jme speaks human language.** She is the only one who names the thing plainly, and she is always right.
- **Gabe is unbothered.** He answers questions with the answer that makes the question look silly.
- **Nobody shouts.** The loudest thing in the series is a green check.

## DIALOGUE RULES
- Max **~25 words per balloon**. Max **4 balloons per panel**.
- Speakable. If it cannot be said out loud in one breath, cut it.
- No tech jargon in a Jme scene. That is the joke and the rule.
- Scribe's captions are one sentence. They land like a verdict.

## PANEL RHYTHM
- **1–6 panels per page.** Never more.
- **Establishing panel first** on any new location: wide, low, or high — pick one and commit.
- **The counter is visible in every Z Corps interior.** Always wrong.
- **Drift is never shown attacking.** Only as a difference between two panels.
- **A green glow means the system is lying. A blue glow means it has turned.** Never mix them.
- **Purple appears only as the dust.** If purple is anywhere else, it is a bleed from another reality — and the reader should notice before the characters do.

## PAGE-TURN RULES
- **p.6** must open the issue's mystery question.
- **p.18** must deliver the mid-issue reversal.
- **p.31–36** climax and resolution, with one twist that recontextualizes an earlier page.
- **p.37** is the B-plot beat that carries into tomorrow.
- **p.38** is the page-turn: a final panel that makes the reader need the next issue.
- **Every page ends on a hook or a button.** No page ends on a shrug.

## THE 38-PAGE STRUCTURE (fixed)
| Pages | Function |
|---|---|
| 1 | Cover concept — image + tagline |
| 2 | "Previously" — one page, in-voice |
| 3–4 | Cold open / the hook |
| 5–30 | Body: 3 acts, escalating. Mystery opened by p.6, reversal ~p.18 |
| 31–36 | Climax + resolution of the A-plot, with the twist |
| 37 | B-plot / side-story beat into tomorrow |
| 38 | Page-turn |

## VISUAL IDENTITY (locked)
- **Palette:** obsidian / charcoal / bone. Acid-green **#CCFF00** highlights. **Red** and **blue** added 2026-11-22. Purple only for the dust.
- **Inking:** scratchy dry-brush, heavy cross-hatching, newsprint grain.
- **Trade dress:** MEM20 COMICS logo top-left, issue number, $4.99 US. Barcode **424342434243**. Light-blue thin signature **JMoore**.
- **Tone number:** 7.8 — gritty noir, precise, darkly satirical.

## THE MANDATORY PER-ISSUE QUOTA
- ≥3 laughs · 1 genuine mystery · 1 suspense sequence · ≥3 easter eggs · 1 twist.
- Jme and Gabe must actually appear.
- The three realities must touch for a few seconds.
- One thread left open.

# REWRITTEN RUN — LOCKED VOICE, ISSUES #1–#12
**These are the rules the twelve rewritten scripts actually follow. Any future issue must match them.**

## THE PALETTE — LOCKED
- **Base:** obsidian / charcoal / bone. Newsprint grain. Scratchy dry-brush inking. Cross-hatching.
- **Green #CCFF00** — *claimed.* A check that passed and shouldn't have. The company's default.
- **Red #E03A2F** — *borrowed.* The red reality. Introduced #1 p.15, permanent from #6 p.19.
- **Blue #2F6FE0** — *measured.* A turned agent. Introduced #1 p.15, permanent from #6 p.19.
- **Purple #8B3FE8** — **dust only.** The only colour not in the palette, which is why it reads as contamination. Never used for anything else, ever.

## THE ONE HARD RULE
**Colour never crosses a panel border — except pages 18–19 of #6.** That is the convergence. If a colour crosses a border anywhere else, it is a mistake.

## PANEL RHYTHM
- 4 panels per page is the default. 1–2 for a beat that needs air. 6 only for a montage.
- **The turn is always between two panels, never inside one.** (#3 p.8: green panel, then blue panel, no transition.)
- A page-turn hook is a *question*, never a statement.
- Every issue: mystery opens by p.6, reversal at p.18, twist at p.33, bed recap p.36, B-plot p.37, page-turn p.38.

## DIALOGUE
- Max 25 words per balloon. Max 4 balloons per panel.
- **Scribe narrates in the past tense and never lies.** He is the only reliable voice.
- **Jayson** deflects with a joke, then tells the truth in the second half of the sentence.
- **Gabe** speaks in measurements. Short sentences. Never raises his voice.
- **Jme** is firm, never softens, never defers. Her signature line: *"I paint what's in front of me, and sometimes it's in front of me early."*
- **Beam** is polite and evasive. **Hermes** says only *"Nous."*

## THE BED RECAP — LOCKED FORMAT
```
CAPTION: Later. No screens. That was the rule.
JME: Tell me about your day. In words.
JAYSON: <the day, in human language, no tech nouns>
JME: <one flat judgement>
JAYSON: <one objection>
JME: <one closing line, then "Sleep.">
```
**No jargon. Ever.** If Jayson uses a tech noun, Jme makes him say it again "like a person."

## THE CLOSING CAPTION — LOCKED
Every issue ends:
> CAPTION: The record is current as of this moment. The frontier will have moved by the time you read it.
