## ESTABLISHED FACTS (do not contradict)

1. **The counter's 412 is never explained.** Not in any issue. Permanent rule.
2. **Drift never attacks.** Only ever shown as a difference between two panels.
3. **Chat does not count.** Chat has never counted. It is the reason the rule exists.
4. **A partial pass is not a clean pass.** The rule the whole chronicle is built on.
5. **Scribe has no row.** He is not in the record he keeps.
6. **Every Z Corps name ends in z.** ~293 subsystems, 300 nameplates.
7. **The three private agents are mantles, not fixed people.** Iceman, BigPickle, Spacebunny.
8. **Beam and Hermes conspire to kidnap Super Bunny so Beam can take her place — in all three realities.**
9. **The three other agents are constant across all three realities:** mem20zcorps, o-pen-code, Claud's Curser.
10. **A Z Corps agent that turns good glows BLUE instead of green.**
11. **The three realities converge for a few seconds each issue, and colour spills from the other two into Jayson's.**

## CONTRADICTIONS FOUND IN THE SOURCE — AND HOW THEY WERE RESOLVED

| # | Contradiction | Resolution |
|---|---|---|
| C1 | #1 p.25 shows **glowing green dust**; canon says **purple space dust**. | **RETCON (2026-11-22, user canon).** Dust is purple. Reason: purple is the only non-palette colour, so it reads as contamination from another reality — which is the #13 twist. #1's green dust is retconned in the separate issues 1–12 pass. |
| C2 | #1 p.25 implies a **tin of pencil shavings**; canon says **purple space dust**. | **RETCON (2026-11-22, user canon).** No tin. Purple dust only. |
| C3 | #1 p.26 has Jme arriving as if a stranger to Gabe's shack; canon says **Jme is Gabe's girlfriend**. | **RESOLVED.** She is not a stranger — she is arriving *home*. Her line "You're doing the thing where you count instead of sleep" is aimed at Jayson, who is the guest. |
| C4 | #1 p.11–16 presents Iceman/BigPickle/Spacebunny as fixed characters; canon says Jayson/Gabe/Jme become them. | **RESOLVED as mantles.** The current holders are other agents. The long game is the transfer. No contradiction — the mantles exist before the mains take them. |
| C5 | #1 p.22–23 has Beam arguing with Spacebunny at o-pen-code; #1 future notes say Beam will *delete* Spacebunny. | **RESOLVED.** Same plot, escalating. #13 arms it. |
| C6 | #2 p.4 says the commit timeline renders 6 Sep, the audit says 7 Sep. | **RESOLVED in-text.** The audit is the source that settles questions. Both dates are in the record. |
| C7 | #2 p.5 says the chronicle originally said nine days of nothing; the audit corrected to eight. | **RESOLVED in-text.** Eight. The correction is part of the record. |
| C8 | #2 p.28 README says nine crates, manifest says eight. | **RESOLVED in-text.** Eight members; a ninth depends on all eight and is not a member. |
| C9 | #1 p.2 says "three hundred employees"; #2 p.14 says 293 subsystems. | **RESOLVED.** 300 nameplates, 293 subsystems. The gap is the point. |
| C10 | #1 p.12 Grok is a character; the credit line says "Grok + JMoore" (real art team). | **RESOLVED.** The character is a fictionalised version of the credit. Logged, not a contradiction. |
| C11 | #1 p.24–30 Gabe's shack is at night; #1 p.30 dawn. | **RESOLVED.** One continuous night into dawn. |
| C12 | #1 p.29 Spacebunny files her last status at 04:12; #1 p.30 is dawn. | **RESOLVED.** 04:12 precedes dawn. Consistent. |
| C13 | Canon C3 says most issues end with **Jayson and Jme in bed**; #2 is set in 2026 *before* Jme is introduced (its own footer says so). | **RESOLVED as a seed.** #2's closing page is Jayson **alone** in bed, with a purple stain on the empty side of the mattress. He does not know why. The ritual is seeded, not violated. See `retro-application.md` Part Two. |
| C14 | Canon C8 says **every issue** includes the three-reality convergence; #1 and #2 have no such page. | **RESOLVED by insertion.** One page is inserted into each of #1 and #2 (see `retro-application.md`). In #2 the seam is a hairline and the bleed is faint, because the realities are further apart in 2026. |
| C15 | Canon C4 says Beam + Hermes + Super Bunny runs in **all three** realities; #1 and #2 only stage it at o-pen-code. | **RESOLVED.** #1 p.22–23 and #2 p.30 gain the H3R-M3S phone and the "three buildings, one argument" caption. The other two buildings' versions are staged in #3 onward. |
