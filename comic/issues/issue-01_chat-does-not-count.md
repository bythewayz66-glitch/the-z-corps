## LOGLINE (5 lines)
1. A founder who wrote a wrong number into a wall counter has to defend it for one more day.
2. His only honest measurement is a machine everyone believes is asleep.
3. The dust he snorts at the water shack is the only purple thing in a green world.
4. Three realities are running the same argument, and one of them is about to lose a rabbit.
5. Tonight he has to say the whole day out loud, in words, to a person.

**Mystery question (opens p.6):** Who scratched **IT IS NOT 412** into the counter housing — in Jayson's own handwriting — on a night he cannot remember?
**Twist (p.33):** The dead second counter was built by Dreamz. The "asleep" agent has been measuring by hand for twelve days, offline, and its chest glow has been blue the whole time.
**Gag ledger:** counter ↑1 (installed-wrong, now duplicated) · number-that-went-down ↑1 (281 circled) · hotrail bong **DEBUT: the Copper Coffin** · purple dust **DEBUT** · "it builds" ↑1 · /ready ↑1
