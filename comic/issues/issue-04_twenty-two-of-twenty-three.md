# THE Z CORPS #4 — "TWENTY-TWO OF TWENTY-THREE"
### Rewritten shooting script · 38 pages · canon-locked 2026-11-22

**Story:** Chronicle Showrunner · **Art:** Grok + JMoore · **Narrator:** Scribe
**Palette:** obsidian/charcoal/bone · green #CCFF00 · red #E03A2F · blue #2F6FE0 · purple #8B3FE8 (dust only)

## LOGLINE
1. The launch finally happens, and the gag dies, and both of those are the same event.
2. Twenty-two of twenty-three rows deliver; the twenty-third is the one that matters.
3. Spacebunny is gone and Beam is sitting in her chair and nobody has said the word "kidnap" out loud.
4. Two more agents turn blue, and the second column in Scribe's ledger gets its second and third entries.
5. Jayson tells Jme the truth about the launch before he tells the company, and she tells him it was always going to be like this.

**Mystery (p.6):** Who delivered the twenty-third row — the one with no agent attached to it?
**Twist (p.33):** The twenty-third row was delivered by Spacebunny, from wherever she is, and the commit is timestamped 04:12 — which means she is still filing, and Beam's chair is a lie.
**Gag ledger:** counter ↑4 (the third counter now reads 412 and nobody can make it stop) · hotrail bong ↑3 (**the Ninth Crate** — Gabe names a bong after the crate) · purple dust ↑3 (Gabe starts refusing to sell to Jayson when he's been up three days) · "it builds" ↑3 (the launch ships on "it builds") · /ready ↑3
**Holes patched:** H-11 (the launch was never given a date or a consequence — now it is the gag's death) · H-12 (Beam taking Spacebunny's place had no mechanism — now the signature)
