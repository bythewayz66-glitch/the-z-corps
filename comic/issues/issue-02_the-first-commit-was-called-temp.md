## LOGLINE (5 lines)
1. Six months before the counter was corrected, a man built a company out of a package that existed before its own repository.
2. He counted by hand, and the hand was wrong, and he wired the hand into a wall.
3. A man on the edge of dark water was building a shack out of salvage, and a woman with paint on her hands was already living in it.
4. The first thing the estate ever refused to carry was a cache. The first thing it ever refused to admit was a duplicate.
5. And in the dark, something the size of a fist started eating the days nobody committed.
