# THE Z CORPS #3 — "THE CLIMB FORWARD"
### Rewritten shooting script · 38 pages · canon-locked 2026-11-22

**Story:** Chronicle Showrunner · **Art:** Grok + JMoore · **Narrator:** Scribe
**Palette:** obsidian/charcoal/bone · green #CCFF00 · red #E03A2F · blue #2F6FE0 · purple #8B3FE8 (dust only)

## LOGLINE
1. The climb forward begins: twelve posts, one launch, and a number that has to survive all of them.
2. Claud's Curser is introduced properly — a company that measures nothing and displays everything.
3. The first Z Corps agent turns good, and its green glow goes blue, and nobody has a word for it.
4. Beam starts keeping a schedule of somebody else's movements.
5. Jayson tells Jme about the blue light before he tells Scribe, which is the first honest thing he does all issue.

**Mystery (p.6):** Why does Claud's Curser's counter read *correctly* — and why is that more frightening than 412?
**Twist (p.33):** Claud's Curser's counter is correct because it is not connected to anything. It is a display. The number is typed in each morning by a person whose job title is "Displayed."
**Gag ledger:** counter ↑3 (a third counter, correct, and fake) · hotrail bong ↑2 (**the Bishop**) · purple dust ↑2 (Gabe starts weighing it) · "it builds" ↑2 · /ready ↑2 (something polls it once)
**Holes patched:** H-09 (Claud's Curser was only a sign in #2 — now a company with a method) · H-10 (the blue turn had no first instance — mem20opsz is it)
